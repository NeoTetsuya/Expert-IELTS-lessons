4a IELTS Essay Prompt Analysis

"Some people believe that it is good for us to spend time in a large crowd. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?"

Advantages (Pros):
  • Generates an intense sense of community, shared energy, and collective excitement.
  • Feeling connected to thousands of people who share the same passion (football, concerts).
  • Reduces loneliness and creates lasting cultural memories.
Disadvantages (Cons):
  • Crush dangers, stampedes, and inadequate crowd control.
  • Noise pollution, claustrophobia, and limited personal space.
  • Increased risk of theft, antisocial friction, or public transit chaos.

Expert Grammar Page 182 Reference Guide

Quantifiers Reference

Comprehensive guide to quantifiers with countable and uncountable nouns, positive vs negative connotation (*few* vs *a few*), and formal academic quantifiers for IELTS.

CU

Countable vs. Uncountable

Noun Compatibility Rules

1. Plural Countable Nouns ONLY:

• many, a few, few, several, a (large) number of

"A large number of events provide a sense of community."

2. Uncountable Nouns ONLY:

• much, a little, little, a (large) amount/quantity of, a bit of

"The sound of cheering generates a large amount of excitement."

3. BOTH Countable & Uncountable:

• a lot of, lots of, plenty of, some, any, hardly any, enough, no

"plenty of room" (uncountable) / "plenty of events" (countable)

FL

Connotation: Few vs. A few

Positive vs. Negative Meaning

1. A FEW / A LITTLE (Positive = some):

Emphasizes that there is a small amount, but it is enough or reassuring.

"A few people attended." (At least some people came).

"We have a little time to finish." (We still have some time).

2. FEW / LITTLE (Negative = not enough / almost none):

Emphasizes scarcity, disappointment, or absence.

"Few children went to the festival." (Hardly any children went).

"There's little time to answer." (Almost no time remaining).

3. Too many vs. Too little:

too many + plural noun (excess): "too many people"

too little + uncountable noun (deficiency): "too little space"

⚡ Quantifiers in IELTS Academic Writing (Task 1 & Task 2)

A large number of

Takes a plural verb: "A large number of spectators were present at the stadium."

A large amount of

Takes a singular verb: "A large amount of money was invested in crowd security."

Formal Replacements

In Task 2 essays, replace informal "lots of" with a significant proportion of, a considerable number of, or plenty of.
