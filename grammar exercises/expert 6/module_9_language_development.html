Expert Grammar Page 181 Reference Guide

Modals of Obligation Reference

Comprehensive guide on using modals to express obligation, necessity, prohibition, and advice—both in the present and the past.

PR

Present Modals

Obligation, No Obligation, Prohibition

1. Obligation & Advice (Do it):

must / have to / need to: Strong obligation.

should / ought to: Advice or mild obligation.

2. Lack of Obligation (50-50 / You choose):

don't have to / don't need to / needn't: It is not necessary, but you can if you want.

"You don't need to get anyone to sign them out."

3. Prohibition (Do not do it):

mustn't / can't / shouldn't: It is forbidden or a very bad idea.

"We mustn't forget to include some photos."

PA

Past Modals

Transforming Present to Past

1. Past Obligation:

must / have to ➔ had to

"Poor people had to make huge sacrifices."

2. Past Lack of Obligation:

don't need to ➔ didn't need to (it wasn't necessary) OR needn't have + past participle (you did it, but it wasn't necessary).

3. Past Advice & Regret:

should ➔ should have + past participle

shouldn't ➔ shouldn't have + past participle

"A lot of people died, which shouldn't have happened."

4. Past Ability / Permission:

can ➔ could

can't ➔ couldn't
