Sentence A Present Simple

"The delicatessen's named after the owner, Daisy."

• Subject: The delicatessen

• Auxiliary + Verb: 's (is) + named

• Agent: Daisy (introduced by after)

• Tense: Present Simple Passive

Sentence B Present Perfect

"The corner shop's recently been sold."

• Subject: The corner shop

• Auxiliary + Verb: 's been (has been) + sold

• Agent: Unknown / not mentioned

• Tense: Present Perfect Passive

Sentence C Future Simple

"Hopefully the market won't be moved again for some time."

• Subject: The market

• Auxiliary + Verb: won't be (will not be) + moved

• Agent: Unknown / not stated

• Tense: Future with will (negative)

Expert Grammar Page 180 Reference Guide

The Passive Voice Reference

Comprehensive guide to passive tense forms, agent usage with by, omission of obvious or unknown agents, and application in IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 process diagrams.

PV

Form & Primary Uses

Subject + be + past participle

1. Focusing on the action or receiver:

Used when the action itself, or the person/thing affected by it, is more important than who performed it.

"The vegetables are harvested from the field."

2. Unknown, obvious or unimportant agents:

• Unknown: "The food market was relocated last year."

• Obvious: "The thief was arrested [by the police]."

3. By + Agent:

Only include by + agent if the agent provides important, specific, or surprising information:

"Most farm animals are looked after by women."

TC

Tense Conjugation Matrix

Active vs. Passive Verb Forms

Tense Passive Form
Present Simple am / is / are + done (e.g. are wasted)
Past Simple was / were + done (e.g. was relocated)
Present Perfect has / have been + done (e.g. has been sold)
Future (will) will be + done (e.g. won't be moved)
Modals modal + be + done (e.g. can be stored)

⚡ Process Descriptions in IELTS Academic Writing Task 1

When to Use Passive

Use the passive for man-made stages where the human operator is irrelevant: "The beans are transported, sorted, and canned."

When to Use Active

Use the active when the machine, agent, or natural phenomenon performs the action: "A machine cuts the beans." or "Water evaporates."

Sequencing Adverbs

Place time connectors naturally between auxiliary and past participle: "They are then placed into hot water..." / "Once cooled, the cans are moved..."
