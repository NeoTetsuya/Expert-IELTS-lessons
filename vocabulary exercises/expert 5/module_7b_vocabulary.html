Unit 7b Glossary Page 113 Reference & Pronunciation Guide

Comprehensive Public Buildings & Services Lexicon

Full reference definitions, phonetic transcriptions, grammatical collocations, and one-click British English pronunciation.

town hall

compound noun [C]

A large building containing local government offices and council meeting rooms.

Collocation: council meeting at the town hall

retirement home

compound noun [C]

A residential institution providing accommodation and nursing care for elderly citizens.

Collocation: volunteer at a retirement home

community centre

compound noun [C]

A building where members of a neighbourhood gather for social events, classes, and recreation.

Collocation: start an art class at the community centre

leisure centre

compound noun [C]

A municipal complex featuring swimming pools, gym equipment, sports halls, and workout classes.

Collocation: work out at the local leisure centre

concert hall

compound noun [C]

A large public building designed specifically for the performance of live music concerts.

Collocation: perform live at the city concert hall

public housing

noun [U]

Affordable residential accommodation funded, built, and managed by the government for low-income citizens.

Collocation: build affordable public housing

environmental protection

compound noun [U]

Public policies and actions dedicated to preventing pollution and conserving nature.

Collocation: budget for environmental protection

tourist information centre

compound noun [C]

An office offering travel guides, maps, event tickets, and advice to visitors in a city.

Collocation: pick up a map at the tourist information centre

guesthouse & B&B

accommodation nouns

Smaller, cozier lodgings offering personal host hospitality and included morning meals.

Collocation: stay in a family-run guesthouse
