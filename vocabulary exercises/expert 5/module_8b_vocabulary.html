Unit 8b Glossary Page 129 Reference & Pronunciation Guide

Comprehensive Activity & Sports Lexicon

Full reference definitions, phonetic transcriptions, grammatical collocations, and one-click British English pronunciation.

championship

noun [C]

A high-level sports competition to find which player or team is the overall champion.

Collocation: win / compete in the world championship

marathon

noun [C]

A long-distance running race with an official distance of 42.195 kilometres (26.2 miles).

Collocation: run / train for a marathon

semi-final

compound noun [C]

One of the two matches that immediately precede the final game in an elimination tournament.

Collocation: reach / knock out in the semi-final

self-discipline

compound noun [U]

The ability to control oneself and make yourself work hard or behave in a particular way without needing anyone else to tell you.

Collocation: require immense self-discipline

self-belief

compound noun [U]

Trust and confidence in your own abilities, judgements, and potential to succeed.

Collocation: unshakable self-belief in victory

personal best

compound noun [C]

The fastest time, highest score, or greatest distance ever achieved by an individual competitor.

Collocation: achieve / set a personal best

beat vs. win

verb distinction

You beat an opponent or team (e.g. beat France). You win a match, game, medal, or prize (e.g. win the match).

Rule: beat an opponent / win a competition

score vs. miss

verb distinction

To score is to successfully earn a goal or point. To miss is to fail to convert a penalty or shot.

Rule: score a goal / miss a penalty

rules & records

sports idioms

Break records: set a new historic standard. Play by the rules: behave fairly and ethically.

Rule: break the world record / play by the rules
