Unit 10b Glossary Page 161 Reference & Pronunciation Guide

Comprehensive Intercultural Communication Lexicon

Full reference definitions, phonetic transcriptions, grammatical collocations, and one-click British English pronunciation.

values vs. beliefs

noun contrast

Values: moral principles and standards of good behaviour. Beliefs: convictions or political/religious doctrines held to be true.

Collocation: moral values / political beliefs

customs vs. cultures

noun contrast

Customs: accepted social habits or rituals. Cultures: whole societal systems of arts, beliefs, institutions, and lifeways.

Collocation: local customs / different cultures

manners vs. behaviour

noun contrast

Manners: polite etiquette in social interactions. Behaviour: general conduct and actions shown to others.

Collocation: have no manners / reward good behaviour

flexible / inflexible

adjective [noun: flexibility]

Able to adapt or be adapted to different situations, changes, or unforeseen requirements.

Opposite: inflexible (prefix in-)

tolerant / intolerant

adjective [noun: tolerance]

Willing to accept beliefs or behaviour that are different from one's own without prejudice.

Opposite: intolerant (prefix in-)

patient / impatient

adjective [noun: patience]

Able to wait calmly or endure difficulties and delays without becoming annoyed or upset.

Opposite: impatient (prefix im-)

polite / impolite

adjective [noun: politeness]

Having or showing respectful, considerate, and courteous behaviour towards other people.

Opposite: impolite (prefix im-)

honest / dishonest

adjective [noun: honesty]

Telling the truth and never cheating, stealing, or deceiving others.

Opposite: dishonest (prefix dis-)

loyal / disloyal

adjective [noun: loyalty]

Giving or showing firm and constant support, faithfulness, and allegiance.

Opposite: disloyal (prefix dis-)
