💡 Grammar & Collocation Insights:

• affect vs effect: affect is a verb meaning "to influence/impact"; effect is almost always a noun ("have an effect on").

• deal with vs cope: We deal with a problem (take active steps). Cope usually takes an object with "with" to mean managing emotionally or enduring stress (e.g. cope with pressure).

• prevent vs avoid: We prevent [something] from happening or prevent it increasing. Avoid requires an active agent doing something to stay away from a danger (e.g. avoid noisy areas).

Comprehensive Vocabulary Lexicon

Full reference definitions, phonetic transcriptions, grammatical collocations, and one-click British English audio pronunciation.

blizzard

noun [C]

A severe snowstorm with high winds and low visibility.

Collocation: be caught in a blizzard

drought

noun [C/U]

A prolonged period of abnormally low rainfall, leading to a shortage of water.

Collocation: severe / prolonged drought

hurricane

noun [C]

A storm with a violent wind, in particular a tropical cyclone in the Caribbean.

Collocation: hurricane warning / hurricane-force winds

polar ice cap

compound noun [C]

A high-latitude region that is covered in immense sheets of perennial ice.

Collocation: melting of the polar ice caps

greenhouse effect

compound noun [singular]

The trapping of the sun's warmth in a planet's lower atmosphere by greenhouse gases.

Collocation: accelerate the greenhouse effect

toxic waste

noun [U]

Waste material containing poisonous chemicals that can cause death or injury to living creatures.

Collocation: illegal disposal of toxic waste
