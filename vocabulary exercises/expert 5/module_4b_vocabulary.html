Unit 4b Glossary Page 65 Reference & Audio Guide

Comprehensive Vocabulary Lexicon

Full reference definitions, phonetic transcriptions, grammatical collocations, and one-click British English pronunciation.

habitat

noun [C/U]

The natural environment in which an animal or plant normally lives or grows.

Collocation: destroy natural habitat

species

noun [singular / plural]

A group of living organisms consisting of similar individuals capable of exchanging genes.

Collocation: endangered species

life cycle

compound noun [C]

The series of changes in the life of an organism, including reproduction and development.

Collocation: the life cycle of a salmon

coast vs. beach

geography contrast

Coast: the whole land along a sea/ocean. Beach: the sandy or pebbly strip right by the water.

Example: a long coast with many sandy beaches

forest vs. wood

scale distinction

A forest is a very large tract covered with trees; a wood is smaller and less dense than a forest.

Example: camp in a small quiet wood

farm vs. grow

verb usage

We farm the land / animals (manage agriculture). We grow crops, vegetables, or plants.

Collocation: farm the land / grow your own vegetables
