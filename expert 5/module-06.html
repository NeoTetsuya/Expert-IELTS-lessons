1. Planned Future Intentions (A)
Used when a decision has already been made before the moment of speaking:
• "I'm going to buy my vegetables at the market because food is fresher there."
• "I'm not going to buy any more fast food — it's too unhealthy."
2. Predictions Based on Present Evidence (B)
Used when we can see, smell, or know something now that makes a future event certain:
• "Look at the prices! This meal is going to be very expensive."
• "This cake smells amazing — it is going to taste delicious!"
⚠️ Intentions vs. Instant Decisions (will)
• Pre-planned intention: "I'm going to cook dinner tonight." (Already decided)
• Instant offer/decision: "I'll cook dinner tonight." (Decided right now)

Task Prompt: People should only buy locally grown food; it is better for individuals and society. To what extent do you agree with this statement?
Task Goal: Present and explain your opinion clearly across 4 structured paragraphs (Intro, Individual Benefits, Societal Benefits, Conclusion).

Today supermarkets sell all sorts of natural and processed foods from all over the world. However, many people think that we should only eat meals we prepare ourselves from products that are grown locally. I totally agree with this statement.

Firstly, we get many advantages from eating food that is grown in the part of the world where we live. Local foods, for instance, taste better because they are fresher. They also have all the natural vitamins and other nutrients that we need. Eating local food means that we cannot have, for example, strawberries at all times of the year, but I think this is a good thing.

Secondly, buying food that has travelled a long way is not good for society. Flying produce to Europe or the USA from a place such as Australia or South Africa contributes to environmental problems like air pollution, one of the main reasons for climate change. When the food arrives at an airport, it then usually goes by lorry to supermarkets, leading to busy roads and stressful driving.

If we just buy food that has come from farms in our local area, our diet may be less varied, but this is not a serious problem. Both we and the planet will benefit.

🌟 Topic Sentence Architecture (Ex 2b)

• Topic Sentence 1: Stated directly at start of Body 1.
• Process Link: "When food arrives... it then goes by lorry..."
• Result: "This leads to busy roads, which is bad for the environment..."

🧩 Paragraph Reordering (Ex 2c)

1. [C] Topic: People who only buy food that comes from their local area have a very limited diet.
2. [B] Example: In this part of the country, for example, they can eat lots of potatoes and carrots but can't have bananas.
3. [D] Result: This means that meals are much less varied and interesting for them.
4. [A] Consequence: It also makes it more difficult for them to get all the vitamins and nutrients that their body needs.

Task Prompt: Buying new things has too much importance in modern society. To what extent do you agree with this statement? Write at least 250 words.
Opinion Stance: The writer strongly agrees that commercialism damages individuals (time/money waste) and society (resource depletion & waste).

It is often said today that people spend too much time thinking about what they are going to buy next rather than more important aspects of life. I very much agree that modern society has become too commercial.

Firstly, I think this focus on buying things is bad for the individual person. If someone is always thinking about when they can upgrade their mobile phone or buy the latest fashion of clothes, then they are not taking the time to enjoy what they actually have. In addition, shopping for new things all the time can be a waste of both money and time. There are certainly more constructive ways to spend time, such as reading, doing sport or being with friends and family.

Secondly, getting new things also has negative consequences for society. For a start, many valuable resources are used to make things that no one really needs. As well as this, wanting new things makes people throw away items like last year's phone or handbag. As a result, there are mountains of rubbish near every city, having a very negative effect on the environment.

Therefore, even though I too enjoy buying new things, I agree that shopping has become too important in modern society.

🌟 Essay Evaluation Checklist (Ex 5)

• Organisation: 4 balanced paragraphs.
• Clarity: Clear opinion in intro, developed in bodies, restated in conclusion.
• Cohesive Devices: Firstly, In addition, For a start, As a result, Therefore.

📋 Cohesive Categories (Ex 4a & 4b)

• Adding: in addition, also, as well as this, moreover
• Showing Result: then, as a result, therefore, so
• Showing Contrast: although, even though, but, however
• Giving Examples: such as, like, for instance, one example is
• Listing Points: firstly, secondly, for a start, finally
