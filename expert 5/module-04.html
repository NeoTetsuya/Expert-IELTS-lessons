🎯 Matching Sentence Endings Rules

1. Identify Connectors: because, so that, if, as, whereas, due to indicate the grammatical relationship.
2. Predict Information Type: Ask yourself: Does the ending need a reason, a consequence, a condition, or a contrast?
3. Eliminate Grammar Mismatches: Ensure subject-verb agreements and tense consistency align across both clauses.
1. Past Simple (Completed Actions)
  • Finished events: Action completed at a specific past time (yesterday, last year, two years ago, in 1960).
  • State & Sequence: Series of completed events (I watched TV and then I went to bed).
  • Questions & Negatives: Did you eat...? / I didn't lose weight.
2. Past Continuous (Actions in Progress)
• In progress at specific time: At 8 a.m. I was working out at the gym.
• Simultaneous background: They were using energy while they were running.
• Setting the scene: The sun was rising and it was getting warm.
⚠️ Interrupted Past Actions
While / When Interruption:
Long ongoing background action (Past Continuous) interrupted by a short completed event (Past Simple).
👉 "While I was walking home, it started to rain."

🌟 Effective Paraphrasing (Ex 3)

Never copy the prompt word-for-word. Substitute nouns with synonyms (pupils ↔ children), convert phrases to adjectives (every day ↔ daily), and change grammatical voice.

Task 2 Prompt: Some people believe that it is wrong to test new medicines on animals. Do you think the advantages of testing new medicines on animals outweigh the disadvantages? Write at least 250 words.

🎯 Core Task Requirements (Ex 2a)

Write about the good and bad points of using animals to test medicines, evaluate which side is stronger, and provide your clear opinion.

⚖️ Ex 2b Essay Type Distinction
Type 1: "Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?"
👉 Asks if there are MORE advantages or disadvantages. You MUST decide which side is stronger and justify your stance.

Type 2: "What are the advantages and disadvantages?"
👉 Only asks you to neutrally discuss both sides without requiring an outweigh stance.

📝 Paragraph Architecture (Ex 3a)

  • Para 1: Introduction (paraphrase + stance)
  • Para 2: Weaker side (disadvantages)
  • Para 3: Stronger side (advantages)
  • Para 4: Conclusion (restate stance)

📌 Role of Topic Sentences (Ex 4a)

  • Topic sentences introduce the main idea of the paragraph.
  • They usually come at the beginning of the paragraph.
  • Supporting sentences provide reasons, evidence, and examples.

📋 Paragraph Ordering Drill (Ex 4b)

1 (Topic sentence): Testing new medicines on people can have serious consequences.
2: Poor people may decide to take part because drug companies pay participants.
3: This may encourage them to agree to things that are not good for their bodies.
4: As a result, they may even become seriously ill themselves.
Prompt: Some people believe that it is wrong to test new medicines on animals. Do you think the advantages of testing new medicines on animals outweigh the disadvantages?

📊 Essay Strategy Notes

Putting the weaker side (disadvantages) in Body 1 and the stronger side (advantages) in Body 2 creates a natural flow into the concluding stance.

Paragraph A: Introduction

Many people think that using animals is the best way to find out if new medicines work. Others, however, disagree, saying that it is not right to make use of animals in this way. Let us consider both sides of the argument.

Paragraph B: Weaker Side (Disadvantages)

It is certainly true that there are disadvantages to using animals to test new medicines. Firstly, it is cruel. Is an animal's life worth less than that of a human? Animals are living creatures too and we should respect them. Secondly, a new medicine may not work in the same way on an animal as it does on a person. So the conclusions the researchers come to may be wrong.

Paragraph C: Stronger Side (Advantages)

However, it is very important to test new medicines. Scientists need to develop new medicines so that doctors can improve the quality of life for millions of people all over the world. Giving new medicines to animals will show that they work well. These experiments are also necessary to make sure that the new medicines pharmaceutical companies develop do not have any serious side effects. It is too risky to experiment with new drugs on humans but if chemists are not able to test them, then sick children and adults will not be able to benefit from new treatments.

Paragraph D: Conclusion

I would therefore argue that although it is not good if animals suffer, the advantages of using them to test new medicines outweigh the disadvantages. It is very important for the future of medical science.

🌟 Band 7+ Key Strengths

  • Clear Outweigh Position: Concludes decisively that medical benefit outweighs suffering.
  • Topic Sentence Anchors: Clear topic sentences at the start of both body paragraphs.
  • Cohesive Flow: Firstly, Secondly, However, I would therefore argue.
